How it works

Work through one lesson per week. Answer each checkpoint on your own, complete the practice task, then click Submit my progress at the bottom of the page. You may use AI after your own attempt; readiness checks are taken without it.

Questions? Post in the bootcamp Teams channel or join lab manager question hours.
